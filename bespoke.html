A Programme for Commissioning

The Bespoke Process

SEIDENHERKUNFT does not sell from stock. Every piece is produced for a specific occasion, a specific institution, a specific gift list. What follows is how the work is done.

§ I — Five Steps, Ten to Sixteen Weeks

From brief to delivered.

The standard timeline for a 30–100 piece corporate commission. State-gift commissions and museum-shop programmes follow related processes, scaled to the brief.

i

Brief

An exchange of correspondence. We learn the occasion, the recipient, the audience, the calendar, and the budget. You receive our questionnaire by reply.

Week 1
ii

Proposal

A short written proposal: recommended muster and form, an indicative quote, a production schedule, and a presentation mock-up. Revisions are usual.

Week 1–2
iii

Sample

For commissions above 50 pieces, a single signed-off sample is woven in Nanjing and shipped for inspection. For framed panels and state gifts, two samples in alternative palettes.

Week 3–6
iv

Production

Weaving on the huā lóu draw-loom at the Research Institute. Five centimetres of brocade per pair of weavers per day. Engraving, packaging, and provenance cards prepared in parallel.

Week 6–14
v

Delivery

Air-freighted to a European address; or, for institutional events, accompanied by the founder in person. All pieces signed, numbered, and registered in the house ledger.

Week 14–16

A note on terms

Sample fees and design deposits are typically settled by PayPal on agreement of the proposal. Production deposits (commonly 30%) and final settlement are by bank transfer; invoices specify SEPA or SWIFT details.

For commissions denominated in CNY, alternative payment routes are available — particularly useful for European institutional clients with established Chinese operations.

§ II — For Museums

Museum programmes.

For European museums whose collections include chinoiserie, East Asian decorative arts, or textile history. We tailor programmes to your calendar, your audience, and your scholarly direction.

i

Exhibition Installations

Curated vitrines or rooms presenting yúnjǐn in dialogue with your existing chinoiserie collection. Loan pieces, reproductions of imperial textiles from the Institute's archive, and bilingual didactic material. Recent proposed themes: Chinoiserie Reciprocated; The Silk That Did Not Travel; Cao Xueqin's Loom.

ii

Weaver Residencies

A master weaver from the Research Institute in residence at your institution, with a portable draw-loom assembled on site. Public demonstrations, school programmes, conservator workshops, and the production of a finished panel that remains in your collection. Two to four weeks; bilingual interpreters arranged.

iii

Museum-Shop Programmes

Exclusive shop editions developed with your curatorial team — tied to a current exhibition or to your standing collection. We handle production, presentation packaging, quarterly replenishment, and seasonal reissues. Margin structure and exclusivity terms on application.

§ III — For Corporations

Corporate gifting.

Anniversary commissions, executive presents, board sets, brand collaborations. The yúnjǐn ground is woven to your house palette where possible; metalwork (clasps, frames, engraved plaques) is finished with your insignia.

i

Anniversary Editions

Limited editions to mark a founding date, a milestone, a merger. Typical formats: a numbered set of 50–100 desk panels, board fans, or small framed brocades. Each piece signed; each box bearing the date.

ii

Executive Gifting

Smaller-format gifts for senior recipients — bracelets, lapel-pin brooches, fountain-pen cases lined in brocade. Particularly suited to ongoing programmes (annual client gifts, partner thank-yous, board-recruitment presentation).

iii

Brand Collaborations

Capsule collections developed alongside your design team — fashion houses, hospitality groups, luxury-goods makers. The Research Institute's pattern archive is opened on a project basis; rights and exclusivity negotiated in advance.

§ IV — For Diplomatic & State Gifts

Protocol-level commissions.

Yúnjǐn has been a Chinese state gift for centuries. The Nanjing Research Institute itself has produced ceremonial textiles for Chinese state visits and museum exchanges abroad. We make that same lineage of object available to European protocol offices, foreign-affairs ministries, and chanceries.

i

State Gifts

Singular ceremonial pieces — woven panels, mounted scrolls, presentation suites — for incoming state visits, treaty signings, anniversaries of diplomatic relations. The acknowledged provenance of yúnjǐn as Chinese imperial textile makes it a culturally legible gift in both East and West.

ii

Honorary Awards

Inserted brocade medallions for ceremonial collars, mounted brocade plaques for honour-roll presentations, and framed-panel awards for civic honours.

iii

Memorial Commissions

Singular framed brocade panels commissioned for ceremonies of commemoration, institutional founding anniversaries, and public-collection accessions. Calligraphic inscriptions woven directly into the brocade; archival framing.

§ V — For Private Collectors

By correspondence, by appointment.

Singular commissions for private collections: a framed brocade panel, a fully bespoke fan, a one-of-a-kind woven scroll. We accept a small number of private commissions each year, by personal correspondence with the founder.

The first conversation is always free. A proposal follows within five working days. There is no obligation; many of our most rewarding commissions began with a tentative letter from a reader of the journal.
